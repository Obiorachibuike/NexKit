# Code of Conduct

NexKit follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions focused on
the work. Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.

To report unacceptable behaviour, open a private security advisory on GitHub or contact the
maintainers through the repository. Reports are handled confidentially.
